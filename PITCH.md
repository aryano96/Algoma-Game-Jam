# Perennial: 5-minute pitch

Run the game live while you talk, full screen with sound on. Start at the title screen. Times are approximate.

## 0:00 to 0:30, the hook

> "In Perennial, one tree is three things. In summer it's shade, in fall it's food, and in winter it's firewood. You can't have all three. Every tree you burn to survive tonight is one you won't have next year."

Press Enter.

## 0:30 to 1:30, spring and the wordless tutorial

- Don't explain anything. Point out that the game is teaching itself: keycaps under the character, then a seed bubble, then a plot bubble.
- Plant two seeds. Let a rabbit come and chase it off.
- Say: "Seeds can only go in the ground in spring. Watch the wheel in the corner: the season is a clock you plan around."

## 1:30 to 2:30, summer and fall

- Summer: step out of the shade and show the health bar draining with the sun icon. Step under a tree. "The trees you planted are your shelter."
- Fill the bucket at the shrinking pond and water a sprout. "If it dries out, it's gone, and it won't be a tree in fall."
- Fall: the sprout turns into a tree. Show the fruit falling and the wind pushing you and rolling fruit away. "Your basket holds six. Whatever you leave on the ground rots when winter comes."

- Summer, once you've shown the heat: walk up to the stray dog and press Space to adopt it. "It costs 4 wood for its house, and that's the same wood you need for the fire."

## 2:30 to 3:30, winter

- Darkness, slow snow, fire light. Feed the fire.
- Walk into the dark to grab glowing deadwood. A wolf howls. Run back to the light, where wolves won't follow.
- If the dog has a house, let it bark a wolf away.
- Walk away from the fire and open the sun jar you filled in summer. The dark turns gold around you. "This is summer in a jar. You paid for it with health in the heat, and now you can spend it on a trip into the dark."
- Chop a tree: "This is the choice the whole game is built on. Three logs now, or shade and fruit next year."
- If there's time, stand on the ice at the end of winter so the cracks show, then step off.

## 3:30 to 4:30, how it meets the brief

- **Theme in the mechanics:** each season has its own rules, resource and hazard, and choices carry from one season into the next.
- **Transitions are gameplay:** sprouts become trees, fruit rots, the ice cracks, dusk falls.
- **Made during the jam:** every pixel is drawn in code and every sound is synthesized live. There are zero third-party assets.
- **Accessibility:** open Options and show remappable keys, the colour-blind palette, three difficulties, hint levels, reduce motion and volume.
- **Runs anywhere:** one HTML file, offline, with keyboard, gamepad or touch.

## 4:30 to 5:00, close

> "Three winters to win, endless after that, and your score is counted in tree rings. Perennial means something that comes back every year. So do the players."

## Likely judge questions

- **Why does the theme matter here?** Take the seasons away and there is no game. Planting, watering, harvesting and burning only make sense in that order, on a clock.
- **How did you balance it?** We wrote a scripted bot that plays full games and ran it at every difficulty. Gentle is reliably winnable, while Normal and Hard punish players who don't use shade or who burn too many trees.
- **What's original?** Most season games swap tilesets. Here, one object (the tree) changes meaning every season, and the strongest move in winter (burn it) is the weakest move for next year. The sun jar goes further: the season itself is a resource you store in summer and spend in winter.
- **What would you add next?** Animals that migrate between seasons, a seasonal map that grows across years, and a two-player mode where one player is the gardener and one is the woodcutter.
- **Was anything made by AI?** Answer honestly. Say it was built with an AI coding assistant if your team used one, and check whether the rules ask you to declare it.
